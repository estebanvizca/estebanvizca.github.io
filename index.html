About

From network racks to data pipelines.

I’m a telecommunications engineer based in Ibarra, Ecuador. I started with routing, switching and lab security, then moved into Python, data pipelines and machine learning. The instinct is the same in both worlds: understand the system before touching it.

At MANSUERA I designed and deployed the inventory system I use every day — PostgreSQL, Flask, backups and a local assistant across roughly 3,000 parts. My thesis put a neural network inside an ESP32-class device to choose encryption on the fly. Along the way I learned to evaluate my own work, including its limits.
